RemoteCareer AI is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed specifically for online and distance education students.

Many online learners struggle with identifying industry-relevant skills, creating learning roadmaps, choosing practical projects, and preparing for job interviews without traditional campus placement support. CareerGPT aims to bridge this gap through personalized AI-driven career assistance.

## Problem Statement

Online and distance learning students often face:

* Limited career guidance and mentorship
* Lack of structured learning roadmaps
* Difficulty identifying skill gaps
* Challenges in choosing relevant projects
* Limited interview preparation support
* Uncertainty about industry readiness

## Solution

CareerGPT helps students by:

* Creating personalized career profiles
* Analyzing current skills and identifying gaps
* Generating role-specific learning roadmaps
* Recommending portfolio projects
* Providing interview preparation assistance
* Tracking career development progress

## Core Features

### User Profile Management

* Education details
* Current skills
* Interests
* Career goals
* Target job role

### AI Skill Gap Analysis

Compare current skills with industry expectations and identify missing competencies.

### Personalized Learning Roadmap

Generate structured learning plans based on target roles.

### Project Recommendations

Suggest portfolio-worthy projects aligned with career goals.

### Interview Preparation

Generate technical and HR interview questions tailored to the user's target role.
